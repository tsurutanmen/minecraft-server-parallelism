Correctness test for "remember empty POI searches" (bots/poitest.js, poitest.py).

Procedure: flat stone floor, 5 villagers summoned with no workstation or bed within range, wait 90 s
(several empty searches, every 20-40 ticks, so the patched servers have remembered "nothing here"),
then /setblock a composter next to them and poll every 5 s for a villager with profession farmer.

Seconds from placing the composter to the first farmer:
  Paper 26.1.2 build 74 (official, unpatched)       6
  Folia 26.1.2 build 8 (official, unpatched)        6
  Folia + both patches (B), run 1                   6
  Folia + both patches (B), run 2                   6
  Paper ver/26.1.2 e4e17fc + POI patch, run 1        6
  Paper ver/26.1.2 e4e17fc + POI patch, run 2       12

The patched servers were really skipping searches while the villagers waited: in the load tests,
the share of main/region-thread samples inside PoiAccess.findNearestPoiRecords fell from 79.7% to 9.3%
(Folia, one region) and from 33.6% to 3.6% (Paper). See opt_jfr_shares.txt.

Note: the first version of this test failed on every server, patched or not, because the composter
was placed over air; a floating workstation can never be reached, so no villager takes it. The floor
fixed that. Results above are from the fixed test only.
